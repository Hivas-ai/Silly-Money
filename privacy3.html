Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Minecoins. Your privacy is at the core of everything we do. This policy transparently explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform to earn coins by completing tasks and offers.

Information We Collect

To deliver a seamless earning experience, we collect the following data:

  • Account credentials email · username
  • Device metadata model · OS version
  • Activity & earnings clicks · coins · level
  • Task completion records offers · rewards
  • IP address & region analytics · fraud prevention

How We Use Your Data

Your data powers the Minecoins ecosystem:

  • Operate the app and process your earnings securely
  • Validate task completions and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Personalise your dashboard, rewards, and recommendations
  • Deliver critical service notifications (only when necessary)
  • Optimise performance, fix bugs, and enhance user experience

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and encrypted during transmission using industry‑standard protocols.

We employ rigorous security measures, including encryption, access controls, and continuous monitoring. While no system is infallible, we are committed to protecting your information with the highest standards.

Third‑Party Services

We collaborate with trusted partners to enhance your experience:

  • Offer walls task verification
  • Payment processors PayPal · gift cards
  • Analytics providers performance insights
  • Ad networks optional rewarded content

Each partner operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their terms for full transparency.

Your Rights

You retain full control over your personal data:

  • Access and review your personal data
  • Request permanent account deletion
  • Opt out of non‑essential communications
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Restrict data collection may affect features

Children's Privacy

Minecoins is strictly for users aged 13 and above.

We do not knowingly collect or store information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently done so, please contact us immediately and we will securely delete the data.

Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal, operational, or regulatory changes. We will notify you via the app and update the effective date below. By continuing to use Minecoins, you accept the latest version of this policy.
