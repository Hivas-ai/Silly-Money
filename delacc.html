What happens when you delete your account?

  • All personal data, including email, username, and profile info, will be permanently erased.
  • Your token balance, task history, and any pending withdrawal requests will be voided.
  • Referral links, invites, and any affiliate credits you've earned will be deactivated.
  • Your account will be removed from our system within 72 hours after confirmation.

How to delete your account – step by step

1

Send a deletion request via email

Compose an email to our support address and include "ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST" in the subject line.

Nebsytechnologies@gmail.com
2

Include the required details

In your email, please provide the following information so we can verify your identity:

  • Your RobuxPrize username or registered email address
  • The reason for leaving (optional, but helps us improve)
  • A brief statement confirming you understand this action is permanent
3

Wait for our confirmation reply

Our support team (Nebsy Technologies) will respond within 24–48 hours to confirm receipt and begin the deletion process. You may be asked for additional verification.

4

Account permanently removed

Once we verify your request, your account will be scheduled for permanent deletion. You will receive a final confirmation email when the process is complete.
