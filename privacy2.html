Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Nebsy. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app to earn coins by completing tasks.

Information We Collect

To keep Nebsy running smoothly, we collect:

  • Account details email · username
  • Device info model · OS
  • Usage & earnings clicks · coins · level
  • Task completion status offers · rewards
  • IP address & region analytics

How We Use Your Data

Your data helps us deliver a better Nebsy experience:

  • Run the app and process your earnings
  • Verify task completions & prevent fraud
  • Personalise your dashboard & rewards
  • Send important updates (only when needed)
  • Improve performance & fix bugs

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and encrypted during transmission.

We use industry‑standard encryption and security measures. While no system is 100% foolproof, we continuously monitor and update our protections.

Third‑Party Services

We work with trusted partners for:

  • Offer walls task verification
  • Payment processors PayPal · gift cards
  • Analytics performance
  • Ad networks optional rewards

Each partner has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

Your Rights

You have control over your data:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Opt out of marketing messages
  • Export your data
  • Limit data collection may affect features

Children's Privacy

Nebsy is not for users under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy occasionally. We'll notify you via the app and update the date below. Continued use of Nebsy means you accept the revised policy.
